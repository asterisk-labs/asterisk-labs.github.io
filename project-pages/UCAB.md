**Understanding Clouds from Above and Below** is a four-year, [UKRI Future Leaders Fellowship](https://iuk-business-connect.org.uk/news/meet-the-future-leaders-fellowships-innovators-shaping-what-comes-next/), delivered in partnership with [The Cloud Appreciation Society](https://cloudappreciationsociety.org/).

The Cloud Appreciation Society aims to foster understanding and appreciation of clouds, and has more than 65,000 members in over 120 countries around the world.

Understanding Clouds from Above and Below will combine images of clouds taken by users of the [CloudSpotter App](https://geni.us/8aWhgBn) with imagery taken from space satellites to better understand cloud behaviour.

Satellite imagery can give us incredible views of huge cloud systems from above. Our work on [Clouds Decoded](https://asterisk.coop/project-pages/project-clouds-decoded.html) has allowed us to see cloud properties in unprecedented detail using [Sentinel-2](https://www.esa.int/Applications/Observing_the_Earth/Copernicus/Sentinel-2) data, but we can’t see what’s going on below the cloud-tops.

By harnessing the power of cloud-lovers around the globe, we can acquire multiple views of clouds from the ground as satellites pass overhead, and build up a 3D picture of how our clouds are behaving in a changing climate.

What we don’t want this project to be is a one way, extractive process where scientists simply take from the contributors. A huge part of the project will be a knowledge-exchange program, where the scientific and wider community can get together in person and online to develop materials so that we can all learn from each other.

The project kicks off in December 2026 and we will be actively looking for people to get involved. More details to come soon, and keep watching the skies!
